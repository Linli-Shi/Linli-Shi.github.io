I am a postdoctoral researcher based at the Department of Mathematics, ETH Zürich, with a joint appointment at ETH Zürich and UniDistance Suisse.

I work with David Loeffler and Sarah Zerbes. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of Connecticut in 2025, under the supervision of Liang Xiao.

My research lies in number theory, automorphic forms, and arithmetic geometry, particularly Beilinson’s conjectures, Euler systems, the Kudla program, and the relative Langlands program.

I am on the academic job market for positions beginning in Fall 2027.

Research
